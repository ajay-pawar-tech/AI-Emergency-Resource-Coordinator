# 🔄 Emergency Response Workflow

The AI Emergency Resource Coordinator follows a structured workflow to analyze an emergency, identify available resources, forecast demand, optimize resource allocation, estimate routes, and present the response information through the Streamlit dashboard.

---

## 1. Workflow Overview

```text
Emergency Incident
        ↓
Incident Analysis
        ↓
Victim Analysis
        ↓
Resource Availability
        ↓
Ambulance Selection
        ↓
Hospital Selection
        ↓
Rescue Team Selection
        ↓
Demand Forecast
        ↓
Resource Optimization
        ↓
Route Estimation
        ↓
Weather Analysis
        ↓
Generative AI Recommendation
        ↓
Notification
        ↓
Streamlit Dashboard
